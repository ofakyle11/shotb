1 · The calendar

MilestoneEst. daysPlanned startPlanned end StatusActual startActual endSlipBlocked by

Working days only — weekends are skipped automatically; a weekend anchor date snaps to the nearest working day. Milestones marked ● sit on the planned critical path: a day lost there is a day lost on delivery. The plan is a template estimate and never changes on its own — the status and actual dates you record here are kept separately, laid over the plan to produce the slip and the forecast, and are the only thing this page treats as fact. Record an end date and the milestone counts as done; leave it blank and it does not, whatever the estimate says.

Awarding a bid commits the amount to the Money Room as an open PO on acct 15000 (Post-production) — once only, even if you press award twice. Bids are the numbers you were quoted, not platform estimates.

4 · Delivery readiness

Readiness is read from the actual end dates you record in section 1, not from the calendar. Mark a milestone done with the day it actually finished and the matching deliverable flips from planned to ready — nothing is ticked in /distribution/ for you. Until then the date shown is a template estimate, and a deliverable nobody has finished is never called ready here.
