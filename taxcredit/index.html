3 · Credit meter

Est. credit = qualified actual + committed spend × qualified-fraction × midpoint of the published rate band — an estimate, not a ruling. The Advisor model applies the same formula to your whole budget sheet total; the delta shows how the real ledger is tracking against that assumption. Credits pay out months after audit — they reduce net cost, not the cash you need up front.
