Click a character chip to start a look for them. Scenes take ranges and A-scenes — e.g. 1, 4A, 6-9.

2 · Story days

Scenes grouped into the days the STORY takes place over — the only order in which continuity has to match. Derived from the script's own time-of-day and its CONTINUOUS / LATER / NEXT DAY cues. Where the script does not say, the boundary is marked uncertain rather than guessed: read those with the script supervisor and set them by hand. A hand-set day always wins and is saved.

3 · Costume plot

Character × scene, ordered by STORY day and cross-referenced to the shooting order from the stripboard, so a supervisor can see what must match what. Cells read #change · look. quick change — a change inside one story day. must match — one change worn across more than one shooting day: photograph it. uncertain — its story day is not stated in the script.

4 · Budget rollup

Piece costs are your estimates until quotes land — verify before relying on the rollup for the cost report.

5 · Photo continuity

Photos are resized and kept in this browser (IndexedDB), attached to the look and stamped with the date and the active project.

Read this before you rely on it. The project vault snapshots localStorage only. The photo references travel with the project and into every .cinamate archive; the photo bytes never do — they stay in this browser. Switching projects therefore leaves them behind. Every photo taken from now on is stamped with the project that took it so they no longer bleed between productions, and the scan below counts what is stranded. Until the vault carries the bytes, the pack file is the only way to move them to another device.
