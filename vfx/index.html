3 · Money — planning estimates vs bids

CodeComplexityPlanning est. rangeBid $Final $Bid vs est.Status

Ranges are internal planning estimates by complexity band — not quotes; real vendor bids always win. Award a bid to commit it to the Money Room under acct 15200 (VFX) as an open PO; each award commits once. When the vendor invoices, put the amount in Final $: that settles the PO in the Money Room as paid, and the board records what the shot actually cost against the raw bid and corrects the band for the next shot of that complexity — after two finals, never on one. Verify bids and vendor scope before relying on these figures — VFX pricing drifts with schedule and iteration count.
