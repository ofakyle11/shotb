Delivery schedule

The checklist reshapes to the buyer — a festival premiere needs five items, a streamer wants the lot. Dim items aren't required for this buyer but still tick off if you have them.

Release windows

An exclusivity clash is an overlap in all three dimensions at once — territory, channel and term. Worldwide overlaps Germany; "USA" and "United States" are the same country; Canada 2027 and Canada 2035 are not a clash. A grant with no end date on record is flagged amber, because until the term is written down nobody can say.

Screener registry

Every screener on the record: who has the picture, which cut, and what the rights state of that cut was when it went out. If a copy leaks, this list is where the conversation starts — and if the cut carries an unlicensed cue or an open high-risk clearance finding, the send is refused until you say why.
