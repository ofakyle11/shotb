4 · Cue sheet — PRO submission

A performing-rights society pays from this document, and it cannot pay from one composer name in a text box. Every cue needs a duration, every writer and publisher named with a share — each side totalling 100% — and a PRO affiliation per party. ISWC identifies the composition, ISRC the recording; both are worth asking the publisher and label for.
